How to play

Lexicon is a daily vocabulary practice for the curious. Each session introduces a handful of words through short, varied exercises — no scores to chase, no streaks to protect. Get something wrong and you'll simply see the right answer. Come back when you feel like it.

Sessions
Quick practice
One word, three exercises. Good for a two-minute break.
Full session
Three words, all exercise types. About ten minutes.
Exercises
Definition Read the meaning and an example sentence.
Synonym Pick the word closest in meaning.
Antonym Pick the word opposite in meaning.
Fill in blank Type the missing word to complete the sentence.
Etymology Learn the word's origin and identify its source language.
Idiom Match a related expression to its meaning.

Wrong answers are just part of learning. No guilt.
